# 1.17.0 — Study navigation and observed progress

The progress screen previously mixed weekly decisions with the full corpus horizon and repeated mastery totals. It now opens with the next session and three independent rings: completed planned sessions, initial accuracy on local questions, and unassisted retrieval after at least 30 days. Historical detail and the full corpus horizon remain available in collapsed sections. Each ring retains its denominator; unavailable evidence is not displayed as zero achievement.

Content selection and search are directly accessible. My Week surfaces saved concept and question sessions independently. The existing biochemistry help flow now prompts an oral causal reconstruction after hiding the explanation.

Observed retention and repeated-error measures use dated attempts with matching question versions and verifiable absence of assistance. Partial answers count as errors. A protected plan endpoint supplies topic closure status; closure is explicitly distinct from mastery.
