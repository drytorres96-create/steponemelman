## Membrane — acceso e inicio

Archivos: `public/images/v170/membrane-768.webp` y `public/images/v170/membrane-1536.webp`.

```text
Create a premium ABSTRACT MEDICAL CINEMATIC image for the hero of a sophisticated minimalist medical learning interface. Pure abstraction inspired by translucent biological membranes seen with advanced microscopy and the luminous layering of medical imaging. One restrained flowing arrangement of extremely thin, soft, semi-transparent organic membranes with delicate folds, subtly curved and overlapping, emerging from darkness in the RIGHT HALF of a very wide frame. They are NOT identifiable organs, not a brain, not a heart, not a DNA helix. Restrained frosted pearl-white and desaturated surgical teal material, subtle subsurface scattering, a precise cinematic side light catching the membrane edges, velvety deep charcoal-green background, quiet volumetric depth, macro photographic realism, elegant high-end art direction. The LEFT HALF is mostly uninterrupted deep charcoal negative space for actual interface typography. Minimal, spacious, atmospheric, refined, almost monochromatic. Only a few broad flowing surfaces, no busy detail, no bubbles, no particles, no stars, no glowing neon, no blue sci-fi aesthetic. Not a product sculpture: no pedestal, no stone, no room, no furniture, no plants, no people, no instruments. No readable text, UI, grids, logos, labels or watermarks. Landscape 3:2. A conceptual medical visual, not an anatomical or diagnostic illustration.
```

Archivos: `public/images/v170/fluid-768.webp` y `public/images/v170/fluid-1536.webp`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a luminous companion image to a dark cinematic abstract medical membrane artwork, for headers inside a premium medical study website. Pure abstract biomorphic form, inspired by the fluid translucency of cell membranes and the layered precision of medical imaging. Only two or three expansive delicate curved translucent membrane surfaces, soft pearl and very pale desaturated surgical teal, with a subtle fine organic microtexture, gently overlapping on the RIGHT SIDE. Airy warm-white background, beautiful cinematic raking light, slight hazy depth and precise delicate shadows. Most of the LEFT SIDE is calm uninterrupted warm-white negative space. Macro photography meets restrained medical abstraction, sophisticated minimal editorial art direction. NOT a recognizable organ, NOT a heart or brain or DNA, NOT a diagnostic picture. No stone, no pedestal, no room, no microscope, no instruments, no bubbles, no glitter, no stars, no colored neon, no props, no words or UI, no watermark. Landscape 3:2. Tactile, subtle, nearly monochromatic and extremely uncluttered.
```
